    Stage Magic on Your Doorstep: Creative Burntwood Project Unveils Jam-Packed Year Two Season

    By Katy Rose Meaney

    Bringing the arts directly into the heart of our local communities is where real stage magic begins. Now heading into its second exciting year, the Creative Burntwood project—led by the Lichfield Garrick Theatre in partnership with Burntwood Town Council and Lichfield District Council—is continuing its mission to enrich local culture, enliven neighbourhood venues, and make live performance welcoming and accessible to all.

    Supported by Arts Council England’s Place Partnership Fund, the outreach initiative follows an incredible first year featuring social media sensation Jonny Cole, top-tier comedy from Manford’s Comedy Club, and high-flying children's circus shows.

    This autumn and winter, the project returns with an even richer programme crafted especially for curious young minds, growing families, and relaxed community outings.

    Younger theatregoers can kick off the new school year on 20th September with Magical Honey at Chase Terrace Community Centre—a spellbinding blend of South Asian dance, live music, and cultural storytelling celebrating the triumph of art over adversity (Ages 5+). Creative hands will love MakeBeth at Ashmole Bowls & Social Club on 11th October, where Shakespeare's classic is reinvented through interactive cardboard crafting (Ages 7+).

    For Halloween, slip on your cosy sleepwear for The Witch Who Couldn't Sleep at Burntwood Memorial Institute on 25th October—a sensory-friendly, interactive pyjama party blending animation, atmospheric lighting, and call-and-response storytelling where children guide the plot (Ages 3+).

    The family programme stretches well into 2027 with non-verbal bunraku puppetry in Secrets of the Selkie Sea (16th January, Ages 6+) and puppet encounters in The Zoo that Comes to You at Burntwood Library (7th April, Ages 5+). Finding budget-friendly sensory adventures for tiny tots can often be tough, but Babies Adventures in Music brings an inspiring, accessible sensory session to Burntwood Memorial Institute on 15th March for just £5 (Ages 0–18 months).

    Grown-ups are equally well catered for with the return of Manford’s Comedy Club (26th September), BBC Radio 2 Folk Award winners Kathryn Roberts & Sean Lakeman (7th October), and spine-lingering ghost stories in Tales After Dark at Burntwood Library (18th March).

    When the festive season arrives, experience a Dickensian classic staged in the atmospheric setting of St Anne’s Church with A Christmas Carol (4th December, complete with optional mince pies and mulled wine), or join Father Christmas’ reindeer caretaker in A Christmas Story at Ashmole Bowls & Social Club (12th December) for an uplifting journey about communication, empathy, and festive cheer.

  • Project: Creative Burntwood (Year 2 Outreach Season)

  • Lead Partners: Lichfield Garrick Theatre, Burntwood Town Council & Lichfield District Council (Supported by Arts Council England)

  • Key Venues: Burntwood Memorial Institute, Chase Terrace Community Centre, Burntwood Library, St Anne’s Church & Ashmole Bowls and Social Club

  • Family Highlights: Magical Honey (20 Sep), MakeBeth (11 Oct), The Witch Who Couldn't Sleep (25 Oct), A Christmas Carol (4 Dec), A Christmas Story (12 Dec), Secrets of the Selkie Sea (16 Jan), Babies Adventures in Music (15 Mar), The Zoo that Comes to You (7 Apr)
