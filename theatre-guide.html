Theatre Guide

Essential venue guides for parents: accessibility, relaxed performances, booster seat availability, and sensory support.

Birmingham Hippodrome

Birmingham

Accessibility: Level access to stalls and circle via lifts, accessible toilets on all levels, booster seats available at kiosk, and Sennheiser infrared hearing system installed.

Relaxed Performances: Regular relaxed and sensory-friendly performances scheduled for major pantomimes and touring family shows with chill-out zones in the foyer.
