Privacy, Data Protection & Cookie Governance Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Our Commitment to You: At Behind the Magic Curtain (BTMC), we deeply value the trust of the families, theatre-goers, and neurodivergent advocates who visit our platform. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy, ensuring absolute GDPR compliance, and maintaining transparent data practices.

1. Data Controller Identity

Behind the Magic Curtain ("BTMC", "we", "us", or "our") operates as a specialist UK family theatre review and neurodivergent accessibility platform, curated and edited by Katy Rose Meaney. For any questions regarding your data privacy, you can contact our data governance team directly at: Hello@behindthemagiccurtain.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect information you provide to us voluntarily, as well as automated telemetry data necessary for site performance:

  • Voluntary Submissions (Sign-up Forms & Newsletters): When you subscribe to our updates, submit feedback via our Google Forms pipelines, or register your contact details, we collect identifiers such as your name and email address.
  • Marketing & Direct Communications: If you explicitly opt in to receive updates, newsletters, or marketing communications from BTMC, we utilize your contact details to send you tailored content regarding regional theatre productions, family guides, special features (such as our Disneyland Paris coverage), and brand announcements.
  • Technical Analytics Data: Via Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-CPHCSFHVJK), we collect anonymized telemetry data concerning page views, user pathways, and site interactions to help us understand which accessibility guides and West Midlands reviews are most valuable to our readers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing & GDPR Compliance

We process all personal data strictly in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Our legal bases for processing include:

  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in via our submission forms to receive marketing or community updates from BTMC. You retain the absolute right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: Operating and improving our digital publishing platform, maintaining site security, and analyzing readership metrics to enhance neurodivergent accessibility resources.

4. Data Security, Storage, and Third-Party Restrictions

Your security is paramount to our architecture:

  • Secure Storage: All collected user details and form submissions are stored securely using encrypted, industry-standard cloud storage and administrative infrastructure restricted via secure credentials.
  • Strict No-Sale Policy: We will never sell, rent, trade, or commercialize your personal data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or external entities under any circumstances.
  • Trusted Processors: Data is handled strictly within our secure publishing ecosystem and authorized infrastructural conduits (such as encrypted Google Workspace/Forms ingestion backends) solely for the purpose of running BTMC communications and editorial services.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under UK data protection law, you possess enforceable rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The Right to Access: You can request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • The Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request that we permanently delete your contact details from our active databases.
  • The Right to Object / Opt-Out: Every marketing communication includes a clear, instant opt-out/unsubscribe link. Alternatively, you can email us directly to revoke marketing consent.

6. Cookie Policy & Tracking Governance

In compliance with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR):

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Used for core platform mechanics, such as maintaining secure session tokens for our administrative hub. These do not require prior consent.
  • Analytics Cookies: Non-essential analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are held in suspense and will only execute if you provide explicit, active consent via our site-wide cookie consent banner. You may change or revoke your cookie preferences at any time.

7. Policy Updates

We regularly review and update our privacy practices to align with evolving digital standards and legal requirements. Significant updates will be published directly on this page with a revised revision date.
