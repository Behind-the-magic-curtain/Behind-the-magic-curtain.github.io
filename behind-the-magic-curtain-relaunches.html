    A New Stage Begins: Behind The Magic Curtain Officially Relaunches

    By Katy Rose Meaney

    When my son turned to me in tears after his very first show in 2019 and whispered, "Mummy, please go behind the magic curtain and ask them to come back and see me," a spark was ignited. Over the years, as our family learned to navigate ADHD, sensory overstimulation, and an enduring passion for live performance, that spark grew into Behind The Magic Curtain.

    Today, we are thrilled to raise the curtain on our completely relaunched website. We have redesigned every part of the platform to remove the guesswork, stress, and anxiety from planning family theatre visits and immersive days out.

    Here is what you will find across the new platform:

    • Sensory Strategies — In-depth parent insights covering sudden sound spikes, lighting effects, queue management, and quiet break spaces for all featured productions.

    • What's On & UK Tours — Up-to-date tracking of West End transfers, regional Midlands venues, and accessible relaxed performances.

    • Venue Accessibility Directory — Step-free access audits, hearing loops, and sensory breakout spaces across regional theatres.

    Live theatre has the power to inspire every child. Our mission is to ensure every family feels confident walking through the foyer doors, finding their seats, and experiencing the wonder together.

    Take a look around the new site, test out the interactive guides, and join our community newsletter below to stay updated on all upcoming productions and relaxed performances.
