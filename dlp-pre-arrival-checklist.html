✨ Ultimate Disneyland Paris Checklist

Family Pre-Arrival Attraction & Entertainment Research Planner

Instructions for Explorers & Families:
  1. Check the Height Badges and Theme Badges to see which attractions match your party's preferences.
  2. Shade or select a traffic light for each ride to record how everyone feels:
    🟢 Happy & Excited | 🟡 Maybe / Unsure | 🔴 Skip / Not for me
  3. Use the dotted note boxes to jot down sensory notes, queue preparation tips, or YouTube reaction notes!
  4. Review the seasonal shows, parades, and character meet & greets at the bottom, ticking the must-see experiences.
